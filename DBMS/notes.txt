Nested queires
    Syntax:
        select col_name
        from table_name
        where col_name (condition) (
            select col_name
            from table_name
            where col_name;
        );















(DCL)
    Revoke and Grant
        Grant: grants privilege to users
        Syntax:
            1.Grant unique privelage:
                grant privelage
                on db_name.t_name
                to 'username'@'localhost';
            2.Grant multiple privelage:
                grant p1,p2,p3
                on db_name.t_name
                to 'username'@'localhost'

        Revoke: removes privelage
        Syntax:
            1.
            revoke privelage
            on db_name.t_name
            from 'username'@'localhost'
